Build My Bag matches your real-world release angle, spin direction, and throwing distance to the exact combination of Speed, Glide, Turn, and Fade—plus the specific plastic blend and gram weight that fits your arm.

How the 4-Number Code Works

  • Speed (1 to 14): Rim width & required launch velocity to achieve full flight.
  • Glide (1 to 7): Loft capacity—higher numbers stay airborne longer for easy carry.
  • Turn (-5 to +1): High-speed stability right after release (-5 flips easily; 0 resists turn).
  • Fade (0 to 5): Low-speed hook at the end of the flight as the disc slows down.
